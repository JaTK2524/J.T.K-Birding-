Jungle Babbler

Argya striata

The Jungle Babbler is a social and noisy bird that usually moves through its habitat in small groups. It has predominantly brownish-grey plumage, a pale underside, and a sturdy appearance. Jungle Babblers are found in woodland, scrub, gardens, farmland, parks, and urban areas with suitable vegetation. They feed on insects, fruits, seeds, and other small food items, often foraging together on the ground and among bushes. Their constant chatter and energetic group behaviour are among their most characteristic features. A group of babblers can seem to occupy an entire patch of vegetation at once, with several birds calling and moving about simultaneously.

Family: Leiothrichidae
