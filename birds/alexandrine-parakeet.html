Alexandrine Parakeet

Psittacula eupatria

The Alexandrine Parakeet is a large, powerful parakeet with predominantly green plumage, a long tail, and a large red bill. Adult males have a distinctive black-and-pink collar around the neck, while females and younger birds do not show the same complete collar. It is found in forests, woodland, agricultural areas, gardens, and even urban environments. It feeds on fruits, seeds, grains, flowers, buds, and other plant material. Alexandrine Parakeets are highly social and often travel in noisy flocks, their loud calls carrying across the landscape. Their strong bills allow them to crack and manipulate surprisingly hard foods.

Family: Psittaculidae
