Pied Kingfisher

Ceryle rudis

The Pied Kingfisher is a striking black-and-white kingfisher commonly found around rivers, lakes, reservoirs, estuaries, ponds, and other open waters. It is famous for hovering almost motionless above the water before plunging downward to catch fish. Its black-and-white plumage, shaggy crest, long pointed bill, and rapid wingbeats make it easy to recognise. Unlike many kingfishers that rely on low perches, the Pied Kingfisher frequently hunts directly from the air, allowing it to cover large areas of water efficiently. It feeds mainly on fish and other aquatic prey and is often seen hunting in pairs or small groups.

Family: Alcedinidae
