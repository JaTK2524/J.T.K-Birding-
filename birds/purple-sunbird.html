Purple Sunbird

Cinnyris asiaticus

The Purple Sunbird is a tiny, energetic nectar-feeding bird that is particularly common in gardens, scrub, woodland edges, and areas with flowering plants. Breeding males develop glossy, iridescent purplish-black plumage that can shine with different colours in bright sunlight, while females are more subdued yellowish-green. Its slender downward-curved bill is perfectly suited to feeding from flowers, and it may hover briefly while taking nectar. Insects and other small creatures are also eaten, especially when feeding young. Its rapid movements, tiny size, and brilliant male plumage make it one of the most fascinating small birds to watch around flowering plants.

Family: Nectariniidae
