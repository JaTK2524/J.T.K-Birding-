White-Rumped Munia

Quick Facts

  • Scientific name: Lonchura striata
  • Family: Estrildidae
  • Size: 10–12 cm
  • Weight: 10–15 g
  • Wingspan: 16–18 cm
  • Diet: Grass seeds, grains, and other small seeds
  • Habitat: Grassland, scrub, farmland, woodland edges, and gardens
  • Behaviour: Highly social and usually feeds in flocks among grasses
  • Activity: Diurnal
  • Nesting: Builds a rounded or domed nest from grass and plant fibres
  • Breeding Season: Estrildidae
  • Clutch Size: 4–7 eggs
  • Call: Soft chirping and twittering calls
  • Lifespan: Several years in captivity; generally shorter in the wild
  • Conservation Status: Least Concern
  • Where to Find: Grasslands, fields, scrub, plantations, gardens, and forest edges

About the Bird

The White-Rumped Munia is a small, social seed-eating bird with dark brown plumage and a contrasting white rump. It usually occurs in grasslands, scrub, cultivated areas, and forest edges and often travels in flocks. It feeds mainly on grass seeds and grains and can be difficult to notice until a group suddenly rises from the vegetation.
