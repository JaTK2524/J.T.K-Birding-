Common Rosefinch

Carpodacus erythrinus

The Common Rosefinch is a small seed-eating finch in which adult males develop attractive rosy-red colouring on the head, breast, and upper body. Females and younger birds are much more subdued, with brownish plumage that provides excellent camouflage among vegetation. The species is associated with scrub, woodland edges, open forest, and areas where seeds and suitable vegetation are available. It feeds mainly on seeds, buds, and plant material, although insects may also be taken. Its seasonal movements mean that its presence can vary considerably from place to place. The colourful male and melodic song make the Common Rosefinch a particularly attractive finch when encountered.

Family: Fringillidae
