Common Hoopoe

Upupa epops

The Common Hoopoe is an unmistakable bird with a long, slender bill, warm buff-orange plumage, boldly black-and-white striped wings, and a magnificent fan-shaped crest. It spends much of its time on the ground, probing soil with its long bill in search of insects and other small creatures. When disturbed or excited, it can raise its crest into a striking crown of feathers. In flight, the broad black-and-white wings produce a distinctive pattern that makes the bird easy to identify. Hoopoes occur in open woodland, farmland, gardens, grassland, and other habitats where soft ground provides suitable feeding areas.

Family: Upupidae
