Oriental White-eye

Zosterops palpebrosus

The Oriental White-eye is a tiny, active bird easily recognised by the conspicuous white ring around its eye. It has greenish upperparts, yellowish underparts, and a short pointed bill suited to feeding on nectar, fruit, and small insects. White-eyes are highly active and often move rapidly through foliage in small groups, searching flowers and leaves for food. They are common in gardens, woodland, plantations, scrub, and urban areas containing flowering plants and trees. Their constant movement and soft social calls make them lively inhabitants of leafy habitats, while their striking eye-ring provides an excellent identification feature.

Family: Zosteropidae
