Black-Naped Oriole

Quick Facts

  • Scientific name:Oriolus chinensis
  • Family: Oriolidae
  • Size: 23–28 cm
  • Weight: 65–100 g
  • Wingspan: 40–45 cm
  • Diet: Fruits, berries, insects, caterpillars, and nectar
  • Habitat: Forests, gardens, plantations, woodland, and parks
  • Behaviour: Arboreal and generally remains in the canopy while feeding
  • Activity: Diurnal
  • Nesting: Builds a suspended cup-shaped nest in a tree
  • Breeding Season: Oriolidae
  • Clutch Size: 2–3 eggs
  • Call: Clear, melodious whistles and fluting notes
  • Lifespan: Several years in the wild
  • Conservation Status: Least Concern
  • Where to Find: Forests, plantations, gardens, parks, and mature trees

About the Bird

The Black-Naped Oriole is a bright yellow-and-black songbird with a dark stripe through the eye and nape. It spends most of its time in trees and feeds on fruit, nectar, and insects. Its melodious whistles are often heard from the canopy before the bird is visible. It is associated with forests, plantations, gardens, and wooded urban areas.
