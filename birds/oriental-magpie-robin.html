Oriental Magpie-Robin

Copsychus saularis

The Oriental Magpie-Robin is a bold and highly recognisable garden bird with striking black-and-white plumage, a long tail, and a lively personality. Males are predominantly black and white, while females are generally greyer and less strongly marked. It is often seen on the ground or perched on low branches, fences, walls, and other exposed surfaces while searching for insects and other small creatures. A characteristic behaviour is the frequent raising and cocking of its long tail while foraging. The species is also famous for its varied and musical song, particularly during the breeding season, when males can become especially conspicuous.

Family: Muscicapidae
