Indian Cormorant

Phalacrocorax fuscicollis

The Indian Cormorant is a dark, streamlined waterbird commonly found on rivers, lakes, reservoirs, ponds, and other freshwater bodies. It is an excellent underwater hunter and pursues fish and other aquatic prey by swimming and diving beneath the surface. After feeding, it often sits on exposed branches, rocks, poles, or other perches with its wings spread to dry. Its long neck, relatively slender body, and dark plumage give it a distinctive appearance among Indian waterbirds. It can occur in groups, particularly where food is abundant, and may be seen diving repeatedly in productive waters.

Family: Phalacrocoracidae
