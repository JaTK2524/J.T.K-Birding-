Oriental Darter

Anhinga melanogaster

The Oriental Darter is a long-necked aquatic bird with a slender body, pointed bill, and remarkable swimming ability. It is commonly found on freshwater lakes, ponds, rivers, reservoirs, marshes, and other wetlands. The bird swims with much of its body submerged, leaving only its long neck and head visible above the water, which gives rise to its snake-like appearance. It dives underwater to catch fish and other aquatic prey using its sharp bill. After feeding, it often perches with its wings spread to dry. Its unusual swimming style and long snake-like neck make it one of the most distinctive waterbirds of South Asia.

Family: Anhingidae
