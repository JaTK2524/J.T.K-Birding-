Coppersmith Barbet

Psilopogon haemacephalus

The Coppersmith Barbet is a small, chunky green bird with a colourful red-and-yellow face, a strong bill, and a surprisingly powerful voice. It is commonly found in gardens, groves, plantations, wooded areas, and towns where fruiting trees are available. Fruits and berries form an important part of its diet, particularly figs, although insects and other small food items may also be eaten. The species receives its unusual English name from its repeated call, which has been compared to the sound of a coppersmith striking metal with a hammer. Coppersmith Barbets often remain hidden among leaves, but their persistent calls can reveal their presence long before the bird is located.

Family: Megalaimidae
