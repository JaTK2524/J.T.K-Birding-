White-Browed Fantail

Rhipidura aureola

The White-Browed Fantail is a small, energetic insect-eating bird recognised by its fan-shaped tail and prominent pale eyebrow. It is commonly found in woodland, scrub, gardens, plantations, and forest edges. The bird frequently spreads and flicks its tail while moving through branches and foliage, behaviour that gives the species its English name. It feeds mainly on insects and other small invertebrates, often catching prey in short aerial sallies from a perch. Its constant movement and tail-fanning behaviour make it an entertaining bird to observe, although its quick movements can make close observation surprisingly challenging.

Family: Rhipiduridae
