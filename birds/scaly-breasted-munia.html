Scaly-Breasted Munia

Lonchura punctulata

The Scaly-Breasted Munia is a small, compact finch with warm brown upperparts and a distinctive scale-like pattern across the pale breast and belly. It is commonly found in grassland, scrub, farmland, gardens, and areas containing tall grasses and seed-producing plants. Grass seeds and other small seeds form the main part of its diet, and the birds often feed in pairs or small groups. They are active and social, moving through vegetation together while giving soft calls. The intricate scale pattern on the underside is particularly noticeable at close range and provides this otherwise small bird with one of its most memorable identification features.

Family: Estrildidae
