Ashy Drongo

Dicrurus leucophaeus

The Ashy Drongo is a slender, agile bird with mostly grey plumage, a long deeply forked tail, and a sharp, slightly hooked bill. It is found in forests, woodland edges, plantations, and other habitats where it can hunt flying insects. Like other drongos, it is an excellent aerial hunter and frequently launches from an exposed perch to catch insects in mid-air. Its quick movements and alert behaviour make it an active presence in its habitat. Different populations can vary in the exact shade of grey, but the sleek body and characteristic forked tail remain useful identification features.

Family: Dicruridae
