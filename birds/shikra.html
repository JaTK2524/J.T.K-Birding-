Shikra

Accipiter badius

The Shikra is a small, agile bird of prey with short rounded wings, a long tail, and sharp eyesight. It occurs in woodland, scrub, farmland, gardens, plantations, and even urban areas where suitable trees provide cover and hunting opportunities. It feeds on small birds, lizards, insects, and other small animals, often relying on surprise and speed to catch prey. A Shikra may sit quietly among foliage or on an exposed branch before suddenly launching into a rapid pursuit. Its ability to manoeuvre quickly between trees makes it an impressive little raptor and an exciting bird for an observer to encounter.

Family: Accipitridae
