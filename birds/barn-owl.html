Barn Owl

Tyto alba

The Barn Owl is a distinctive nocturnal bird of prey recognised by its pale plumage and remarkable heart-shaped facial disc. It occurs in open countryside, farmland, grassland, villages, and areas where suitable buildings or cavities provide shelter and nesting sites. Barn Owls hunt mainly at night and rely heavily on their exceptionally sensitive hearing to locate prey in darkness. Small mammals form an important part of their diet, although they may also take other small animals. Their flight is unusually quiet because of specialised feather structures that reduce the sound produced by their wings. The pale face and ghostly appearance of the Barn Owl have made it a memorable bird in folklore across many cultures.

Family: Tytonidae
