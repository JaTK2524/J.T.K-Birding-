J.T.K Birding Log

Welcome to J.T.K Birding Log, a place where bird lovers find their kind.
At J.T.K Birding Log, we hope to inform the world about the unnoticed and unappreciated feathered musicians of Earth. So, we created a website for birders to research birds as long as they want, no subscription, no paywall. We hope that our soon-to-be interactive website will attract all people, leading them to learn about birds.
